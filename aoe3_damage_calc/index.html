Help This is a unit stats and damage calculator for aoe3. Select a civ and unit on the left. Technologies, cards, team cards and native techs will appear below. Click each technology or card to activate them in the stats calculation below. Do the same for the unit you're attacking on the right. In the centre a table will appear showing the damage calculation. The page's address keeps the civs, units, cards and auras you've chosen, so you can share a matchup by sharing the link.
